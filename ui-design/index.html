Readable, familiar, welcoming.

Segoe UI typography · 48 px primary controls · 8 px spacing rhythm · soft 12–22 px corners · text-labelled statuses. Light backgrounds support daylight use and clear information hierarchy.

Three roles. One visual language.

Farmers see their harvest and decisions. Buyers see availability and agreements. Administrators see queues, measurements and payment evidence. No auction rankings or automatic payment claims.
